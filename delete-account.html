Delete Your Account

Users of HisabFlow can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Developer: Md Raiqibul Islam Masum

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open HisabFlow.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to the account, profile, or settings section.
  4. Use the account deletion option if it is available inside the app.
  5. You can also request account deletion using the email option below.

Request Account Deletion by Email

To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email from the email address connected to your account.

Email: masumtech.dev@gmail.com

Email subject: HisabFlow - Account Deletion Request

Please include the Google or email account that you use with HisabFlow so that we can identify your account and process the deletion request.

Request Account Deletion

Data That Will Be Deleted

When your account deletion request is completed, data associated with your HisabFlow account will be deleted where applicable, including:

  • Account information associated with the app
  • User profile information stored by the app
  • Cloud-synced app data associated with your account
  • Backup data associated with your account, where applicable

Data Retention

Data that is not required to be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purposes will be deleted after the account deletion request has been processed.

If any information must be retained for legal or security reasons, it will only be retained for the period required for those purposes.

Important Information

Account deletion is permanent. After deletion is completed, your account and associated data may not be recoverable.
